Account Management

Learn how to manage your accounts, including creating, viewing, and editing account information.

  • How to Create a New Account: Go to the 'Accounts' section and click on 'Create Account'. Fill in the required information and submit.
  • How to View Your Account Details: Navigate to 'Accounts' and select the account you wish to view.
  • How to Edit Account Information: Click on 'Edit' next to your account details and make the necessary changes.

Transactions

Find out how to perform various transactions using the system.

  • How to Make a Transaction: Go to the 'Transactions' section, select the type of transaction, and follow the prompts to complete.
  • How to View Transaction History: Access the 'Transactions' tab and select 'View History' to see your past transactions.

Loans

Get information on applying for and managing loans.

  • How to Apply for a Loan: Visit the 'Loans' section, fill out the application form, and submit your request.
  • How to View Your Loan Status: Go to 'Loans' and select 'View My Loans' to check the status of your applications.

ATM Services

Learn about the ATM services offered through the system.

  • How to Use ATM Services: Navigate to 'ATM Services' to find nearby ATMs and check your transaction limits.

Troubleshooting

Here are solutions to common issues you may encounter.

  • Issue: Unable to Login: Ensure you are entering the correct username and password. If you forgot your password, click on 'Forgot Password' to reset it.
  • Issue: Transaction Failed: Check your account balance and ensure you have sufficient funds for the transaction.
